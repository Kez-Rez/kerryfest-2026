THE 40th ANNUAL
FESTIVAL OF KERRY

📅 DATE: Saturday February 14

🕐 TIME: Anytime between 1pm-8pm

📍 VENUE: The Clubhouse Moorooka

There have been many great KerryFests over the years from boat rides, to craft-a-noons, at fancy cocktail bars, squished into karaoke rooms, or dancing amongst a sea of balloons in a garage, and one thing is for sure, they are always a fantastic time!

This year I will be hosting my birthday in The Koala Room at The Moorooka Club house from 1pm-8pm (the entirety of their opening hours, but come along anytime during that time for as long as you like). Beyond the pinball (which is already there just outside the function room), there will be lawn bowls, crafts, room to generally mingle and even some tables to play board games at if you feel inclined.

There will even be some (definitely optional) mini social games — I don't know what they are yet, but you can choose your house below and hopefully I'll come up with something before Valentine's!

Wear whatever you feel your best in; whether it's something that makes you feel fancy, something that makes you stand out, or something that you picked up off your bedroom floor.

Tickets are limited in the same sense that my love for karaoke is, so feel more than free to bring that good friend who makes the events you go to even better.

For crafts, I'll get some basic stations set up, but you can bring what you are currently working on. See some recommendations here.
