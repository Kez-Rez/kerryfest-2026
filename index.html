The 40th Annual
Festival of Kerry

📅 DATE: Saturday February 14

🕐 TIME: 1pm-8pm

📍 VENUE: The Clubhouse Moorooka

There have been many great KerryFests over the years. From boat rides, to craft-a-noons, fancy cocktail bars, squishing into karaoke rooms, or dancing amongst a sea of balloons in a garage, and they all have one thing in common; they are always a fantastic time!

This year I will be hosting my birthday in The Koala Room at The Moorooka Club house from 1pm-8pm.

There will be pinball, lawn bowls, crafts, room to mingle, and even some tables for board games if you feel so inclined.

There will even be some (optional) mini social games — I don't know what they are yet, but you can choose your house below and hopefully I'll come up with something before Valentine's!

Wear whatever you feel your best in; whether it's something that makes you feel fancy, something that makes you stand out, or something that you picked up off your bedroom floor.

Tickets are limited in the same sense that my love for karaoke is, so feel free to bring that good friend who makes events you go to even better.

For crafts, I'll get some basic stations set up, but you can bring anything you are currently working on. See some recommendations here.
